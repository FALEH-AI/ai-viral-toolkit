Our Mission

AI Viral Toolkit was created with one simple goal: to help content creators succeed on YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and Instagram Reels without needing expensive tools or years of experience.

We believe that everyone deserves access to professional-grade content creation tools. That's why we built a completely free platform that generates viral hooks, complete scripts, and SEO-optimized metadata in seconds.

What We Offer

  • 🎣 Hook Generator: Generate 10 viral hooks across 8 niches and 6 tones. Perfect for the first 3 seconds of your Short that determine whether viewers keep watching or scroll away.
  • 📝 Script Writer: Write complete, ready-to-use scripts for 15, 30, 45, or 60-second videos. Each script includes a hook, problem, solution, and call-to-action with precise timestamps.
  • 🏷️ SEO Optimizer: Generate optimized titles, descriptions, and tags that help your videos rank higher in YouTube search results and get recommended to more viewers.

Why We Built This

Creating viral short-form content is harder than it looks. The difference between a video that gets 100 views and one that gets 1 million views often comes down to the hook — the first 3 seconds.

We analyzed thousands of viral Shorts to understand what makes them work. The patterns we found are built into every tool on this site. Whether you're a beginner or an experienced creator, our tools save you hours of brainstorming and planning.

Our Commitment

AI Viral Toolkit will always be free. No sign-up required. No hidden fees. No premium tiers. We believe in making content creation accessible to everyone, everywhere.

Our tools work entirely in your browser — your data never leaves your device. We don't track your searches, store your content, or require any personal information.

Who We Serve

We serve content creators in the Wealth & Money, Alpha Mindset, Fitness & Health, AI & Tech, Business, Relationships, Productivity, and Motivation niches. Our tools are designed for the global English-speaking market, with optimized content for audiences in the United States, United Kingdom, and worldwide.
